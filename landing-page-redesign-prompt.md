# Landing Page Redesign Prompt
> Use this with any AI (v0, Cursor, Claude, etc.) to eliminate AI-slop and rebuild a landing page that feels made by a thoughtful human designer.

---

## Copy Rules

**1. Remove all eyebrow labels / section badges.**
No small uppercase text announcing what kind of section it is ("WHY IT MATTERS", "THE PROBLEM", "OUR SOLUTION"). If the heading needs a label to make sense, rewrite the heading until it doesn't.

**2. Every headline must name a specific actor and a specific outcome.**
"Predict landslides 72 hours before they happen" is specific. "Intelligence built for those who need to act early" is not — cut it.

**3. Every stat must have a one-line source or context directly beneath it.**
If you can't source a number, remove it.

**4. CTAs must be verb + specific outcome.**
"Get early access" is fine. "Learn more" is not.

**5. No section title should follow the formula: [vague adjective] + [product category].**
"Intelligent monitoring." "Seamless integration." "Powerful detection." — all cut. Replace with a sentence that makes a falsifiable claim.

---

## Layout Rules

**6. Maximum two feature grids with icons on the entire page.**
Every other proof or feature section must use a different layout: a single large visual with annotations, a before/after split, a timeline, a quoted case study, or a demo embed.

**7. Every section must have a different vertical rhythm from the one above it.**
Alternate between:
- **Tight** — minimal padding, dense content
- **Airy** — lots of whitespace, single focus
- **Full-bleed** — edge-to-edge visual or color block

No two consecutive sections should have the same density.

**8. Stats and social proof must appear within the first two sections.**
Move the "Powered By" logos to directly below the hero or below the first proof section — not at the bottom.

**9. No section should have more than one CTA button.**
Pick the one action you want the user to take and remove the rest.

**10. At least one section must have no heading at all.**
Let the visual or the data speak entirely on its own.

---

## Structural Rules

**11. The page must have no more than 7 sections total.**
For every section ask: does this answer a specific objection a skeptical user has at this point in the scroll? If not, cut it or merge it.

**12. The section order must follow this logic, in this exact order:**

| # | Section | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Hero** | What it does + who it's for |
| 2 | **Proof** | Stats, logos, or a real result |
| 3 | **Problem** | The one specific problem it solves |
| 4 | **Mechanism** | How it works — not a feature list |
| 5 | **Differentiation** | Why this and not alternatives |
| 6 | **Social proof** | Real words from a real person |
| 7 | **CTA** | What happens after they click + friction reducer |

**13. At least one section must contain only a single sentence or number.**
No subheading, no supporting copy, no icon grid. Make that sentence or number the most important thing on the page.

---

## Tone Rules

**14. Read every sentence aloud.**
If it sounds like it was written by someone who has never used the product, rewrite it from the perspective of a user who has.

**15. Remove every instance of these words:**
- seamless
- powerful
- robust
- leverage
- cutting-edge
- next-generation
- unlock
- empower
- revolutionize
- AI-powered *(unless it's the actual product category)*
- built for

**16. At least three sentences on the page must be under 6 words.**
Bluntness builds trust.

---

## How to Use This Prompt

Paste everything above into your AI tool of choice, then append this to the end:

> Apply all 16 rules before showing me any output. Show me the revised section structure and headline for each section first, before writing full copy or code.

**Why that last line matters:** forcing a section-by-section outline before execution stops the AI from regenerating the same template with different words.
