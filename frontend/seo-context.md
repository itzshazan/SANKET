1. Executive Positioning

Zobique Labs should not position itself as a generic "AI developmentcompany" or low-cost "AI automation agency."

The stronger positioning is:

Production AI Systems for Business

Recommended positioning statement:

Zobique Labs designs, builds and deploys AI agents, intelligentworkflows and private AI systems that automate real businessoperations.

The strategic focus should be on:

AI Agent Development

The core idea is to sell business systems and outcomes, not modelsor generic software development.

2. Strategic Market Thesis

Enterprise AI is moving from experimentation toward productiondeployment.

Businesses increasingly want AI that can:

understand company data

reason over business context

use tools and APIs

execute workflows

interact with CRM/ERP systems

process documents

handle customer conversations

qualify leads

support employees

maintain auditability

operate with permissions and security

integrate into existing infrastructure

Therefore, Zobique Labs should position itself around:

AI systems that actually operate inside a business.

Avoid making the core positioning about:

ChatGPT integrations

generic chatbots

prompt engineering

simple Zapier/Make automations

generic website development

"we use the latest AI models"

Those are increasingly commoditized.
